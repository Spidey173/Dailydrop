Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about delivery, orders, or options? Find immediate answers below.

How do I place an order?

Browse our fresh categories catalog, add your preferred items to your cart, and click Proceed to Checkout in the cart drawer. Specify your shipping details, confirm your phone number, and place your order in seconds.

What are the delivery charges?

We charge a nominal delivery fee of Rs. 30 for small orders. For orders above Rs. 500, delivery is completely FREE!

How long will my order take to arrive?

We pride ourselves on lightning-fast speed! All orders are typically packaged and delivered by our partners within 10 to 15 minutes of placing your order.

What payment methods do you support?

Currently, we support Cash on Delivery (COD) as our primary checkout payment method. You can pay with physical cash, or scan delivery partner UPI QR codes when they arrive.

What is your refund and return policy?

If you receive any damaged produce, incorrect products, or stale items, please raise a support ticket or contact us within 2 hours of delivery to get an instant replacement or refund.

How do I contact customer support?

You can shoot us an email at support@dailydrop.com, submit the contact form on our contact page, or call our customer helpline at +91 98765 43210.
